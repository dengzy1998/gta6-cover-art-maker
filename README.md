Turn your photos and a caption into GTA-style cover art: a Vice City sunset poster with bold outlined lettering, exported as a PNG to share. Everything runs offline in the browser — photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded.

- **Cast Poster layout** — a seven-panel cinema one-sheet: a big hero close-up in the middle (top band on portrait posters) with six supporting panels around it, every panel stamped with an editable role chip (THE BOSS, THE SIREN, THE WHEELMAN…). A review strip runs across the top — five gold stars, a pull-quote and a VOL. 1 box — over a chrome-gradient title with a neon script subtitle and a tagline ribbon, and the bottom carries a tiny-caps billing block, a COMING SOON tag, a barcode and a gold TOP PICK award seal.
- **Classic 3-panel poster** — one big hero panel plus two smaller ones, cut on the diagonal with white seams, in the style of GTA posters. Flip the hero to the other side with one click.
- **Any photo** — load up to seven photos, assign each to a panel, fill all panels with one photo, and crop each one: drag it on the canvas, scroll to zoom, or use the sliders. Seven samples ship with the app, four of them portraits generated with an image model.
- **Bold lettering** — a huge gradient-filled, outlined title with a drop shadow, plus a subtitle line and a place tag. System fonts only; no borrowed logo, artwork, or font.
- **Sunset / neon grade** — a Vice City sunset sky under the panels, a striped retro sun, halftone dots, palm silhouettes, and a vignette. Three grades: Sunset, Neon, Noir.
- **Fast to PNG** — pick a post size (1080×1080, 1080×1350, 1920×1080) and hit Export PNG. The preview canvas already paints at export resolution.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080>. No build step, no network requests.
